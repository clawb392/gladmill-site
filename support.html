Support

Email support@gladmill.com. We reply within 2 business days.

Please include: the app name, your site (yoursite.atlassian.net), what you did, what you expected, and any error message shown in the app. Don't send passwords or tokens.

Security issues

Report security problems to the same address with "Security" in the subject. We acknowledge within 2 business days and fix confirmed issues as a priority.
