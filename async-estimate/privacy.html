Privacy policy: Async Estimate for Jira

Last updated 2026-10-04

Async Estimate for Jira ("the app") is provided by Gladmill and runs on Atlassian Forge.

What the app stores

In Atlassian's Forge app storage for your site: estimation rounds per issue, and each vote with the voter's Atlassian account id, the value and when it was cast; for revealed rounds, who revealed it and who applied the estimate. The app stores no names or email addresses (names are looked up by your browser, through Jira, when you view a round).

Where data goes

Nowhere outside Atlassian. The app makes no external requests and uses no analytics, cookies or tracking.

Account closure and deletion

The app reports the account ids it stores to Atlassian every week through Atlassian's personal data reporting. When Atlassian reports an account as closed, the app erases that person's votes and removes them from revealed rounds. Starting a new round deletes the previous round's votes. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian soft-deletes its stored data and permanently deletes it after Atlassian's standard retention period; if you reinstall within 21 days, Atlassian can restore it on request.
