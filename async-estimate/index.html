Async Estimate for Jira

Team members estimate an issue on their own time. Votes stay hidden until someone reveals them; the team then talks only about the issues where votes disagree.

Vote on an issue

Open an issue and find the Async Estimate panel. Pick a card (Fibonacci or T-shirt sizes). You see who has voted, never their values, until someone clicks Reveal.

After reveal

  • Agreement means a spread of at most one step on the scale, with nobody voting "?".
  • The suggestion is the upper median of the known votes. Set story points writes it to Jira's story points field, using your own Jira permissions. This works for Fibonacci rounds; T-shirt sizes can't be written to the numeric field, so those rounds have no button.
  • New round clears the votes and starts again.

Estimation board

Each project gets an Estimation board page listing issues being estimated. Reveal all reveals every open round; revealed issues are sorted by disagreement so refinement starts with the ones that need discussion. You only see issues you can view in Jira.

Troubleshooting

  • "Set story points" fails: you need edit permission on the issue, and the story points field must be on the issue's edit screen.
  • "This Jira site has no Story Points field": add a story points field to the site (Jira admin), or estimate without writing back.
  • "licence isn't active": a site admin checks the app under Manage apps.
